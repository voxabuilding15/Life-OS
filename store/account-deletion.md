# Delete your Life OS account (publish this page; Google Play requires a public deletion URL)

**In the app (fastest):** Settings -> Delete account -> confirm. Your account, profile, synced content, profile photo and push token are permanently deleted immediately.

**Without the app:** email <support email> from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my Life OS account". We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted: account (email, name), profile, tasks, habits and check-ins, goals, notes, journal entries, focus sessions, achievements, profile photo, push token.
What is kept: nothing linked to you, except records we must retain by law (for example purchase records held by Google Play).
Subscriptions are managed by Google Play: cancel in Play Store -> Payments & subscriptions -> Subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.
