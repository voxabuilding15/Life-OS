Your life. Organized.

Life OS is a personal operating system for students, developers, creators and ambitious people who want to improve every day. Tasks, habits, goals, focus and journaling live in one calm, beautiful dark interface, and every win levels you up.

YOUR LIFE SCORE
One number from 0 to 100 shows how well you looked after your week: habits, tasks, focus, goals and reflection. See what shapes it and the next best move.

DAILY CHALLENGES
Three fresh challenges every day. Finish them for bonus XP, and clear them all for a perfect day.

TASKS THAT FEEL GOOD
Categories, priorities, due dates and reminders. Today, Upcoming and Completed views. Check a task off and watch the XP roll in.

HABITS AND STREAKS
Daily check-ins, streak counters, a calendar heatmap and statistics. Build routines like reading, gym, coding, water and meditation.

GOALS WITH MILESTONES
Break big goals into milestones, follow the timeline and celebrate when you achieve them.

FOCUS MODE
A distraction-free Pomodoro timer with sessions, statistics and history.

NOTES AND JOURNAL
Notes with categories, search and favorites, plus a daily journal with mood.

LEVEL UP
Earn XP, climb levels and unlock achievements across tasks, habits, focus, goals and more.

WEEKLY REPORT
Every week, see how you did compared with the last one.

OFFLINE FIRST
Everything works without a connection and is saved on your device.
