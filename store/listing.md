Life OS is the all-in-one habit tracker, to-do list, goal planner and focus timer for students, developers, creators and ambitious people. One calm, beautiful dark app that shows how you are really doing, and rewards every step forward.

SMART TO-DO LIST
Categories, priorities, due dates and reminders, with Today, Upcoming and Completed views. Finish a task and earn XP.

Turn big ambitions into milestones. Follow the timeline, see your progress and celebrate every achievement.

FOCUS TIMER (POMODORO)
A distraction-free timer with sessions, statistics and history.

Three new challenges every day for bonus XP. Clear them all for a perfect day.

NOTES AND JOURNAL

LEVELS AND ACHIEVEMENTS

WEEKLY REPORT

WORKS OFFLINE
