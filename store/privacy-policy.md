**Who we are.** Life OS ("we") is operated by <legal entity>, <address>, <contact email>.

## What we collect
- **Account data:** email address and a display name when you create an account (Supabase Auth).
- **Profile data:** your chosen focus areas, XP and level, and an optional profile photo you upload.
- **Your content:** tasks, habits and check-ins, goals and milestones, notes, journal entries, focus sessions and achievements. On the Free plan this stays on your device. With **Life OS Pro** cloud sync, it is also stored in your account on our Supabase database.
- **Purchase data:** subscription status through Google Play and RevenueCat. We never see your payment card.
- **Device data:** a push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can deliver notifications, and an anonymous purchase identifier.

We do **not** run ads, sell personal data, or use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

## How we use it
To run the app (sign-in, sync, reminders), to provide Pro features, and to keep the service secure.

## Who processes it
Supabase (database, authentication, file storage), Google Firebase (notifications), RevenueCat and Google Play (subscriptions). They process data on our behalf.

## Security
Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Row Level Security ensures a user can only access their own rows.

## Your choices
- **Delete your account and all synced data** in Settings -> Delete account. This is permanent.
- You can disable notifications in Settings or in Android settings.
- Contact us to request a copy of your data.

## Children
Life OS is intended for users aged 13 and over.
